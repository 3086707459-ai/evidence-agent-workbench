# Gateway procurement requirements / 演示项目需求

This is a fictional evaluation fixture, not a real supplier quote. All prices below are synthetic USD figures for one device, exclude tax and shipping, and are not purchasing advice.

The deployment requires offline operation, local audit logs, and at least a three-year support plan. Any recommendation must cite supplier evidence and list unresolved acceptance criteria.

The three-year cost model is purchase price plus three times the annual support charge. Physical-device latency, long-term reliability, and security certifications require independent acceptance tests.
