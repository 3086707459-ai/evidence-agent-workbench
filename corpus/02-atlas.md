# Atlas gateway / 虚构方案 A

Atlas purchase price is 1200 USD per device and annual support is 240 USD. Atlas supports offline operation and writes local audit records. The stated support period is three years.

The vendor reports a 24 millisecond acquisition latency in its own lab. The report does not provide independent certification or a raw test dataset. This number is a vendor claim, not a measured result from this project.
